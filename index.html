QRStamp

Type text or a URL. Get a live QR code you can download as PNG or SVG. Everything stays in your browser.

Enter text above to generate a QR code.

How it works

QR codes are generated entirely in your browser with qrcode-generator (vendored). Higher error-correction levels make denser codes that survive more damage or logo overlays. Nothing is sent to a server.
